## Brand & Style
The design system manifests precision finance empowered by autonomous intelligence. Geared toward next-generation founders, tech-native operators, and institutional innovators, the interface balances executive gravitas with kinetic computational clarity.

The visual direction merges **High-Contrast Modernism** with **Engineered Glass & Tonal Architecture**:
- Structural surfaces rely on deep obsidian anchors, pristine light canvases, and razor-sharp line weights to communicate balance sheets, ledger states, and liquidity workflows.
- Generative AI capabilities are woven directly into financial telemetry using focused luminescence, providing contextual agent actions without novelty or visual noise.
- Every viewport emphasizes instantaneous scannability, mathematical alignment, and high data density, delivering calm control over complex monetary streams.

## Colors
The palette leverages a crisp architectural hierarchy to separate context, action, and intelligent automation:

- **Canvas & Structural Ground:** The default viewport canvas utilizes Pearl White (`#F8FAFC`), while primary surface cards rest on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Structural navigation bars, persistent header docks, and primary system banners use Obsidian Slate (`#0F172A`) with high-contrast text (`#FFFFFF` and `#94A3B8`).
- **Primary & Interactive Core:** Electric Indigo (`#4F46E5`) defines deterministic intent—commit flows, transfers, and activated states—paired with `#4338CA` on hover and deep indigo tonal fills.
- **Autonomous & AI Accents:** Luminous Mint (`#10B981`) designates computational confidence, AI predictive forecasts, auto-reconciliation, and conversational agents.
- **Status & Telemetry Tokens:**
  - *Verified / Settled / Optimal:* Soft Mint ground (`#D1FAE5`) pairing with Emerald typography (`#059669`).
  - *Flagged / Anomaly / Action Required:* Soft Peach ground (`#FFEDD5`) pairing with Burnt Orange typography (`#C2410C`).
  - *GenAI Synthesized / Agent Active:* Soft Indigo ground (`#E0E7FF`) pairing with Deep Indigo typography (`#4338CA`).
- **Dividers & Structural Borders:** Ultra-fine Slate (`#E2E8F0`) provides structural containment without visual friction.

## Typography
Typography is executed exclusively with Inter, configured with tabular figures (`tnum`) and slashed zeros for financial balances, ledger rates, and dynamic generative output.

- **Financial Display:** Numerical metrics prioritize horizontal density and strict baseline alignment. High-impact balances drop tracking progressively to maintain structural coherence against adjacent action triggers.
- **Hierarchy of Guidance:** AI inferences, dynamic reasoning summaries, and prompt transcripts use `body-md` with regular weights, separated from authoritative ledger values rendered in bold semi-tight tabular formats.
- **Micro-Labels:** Metadata tags, ticker indicators, and variance badges use uppercase `label-sm` with widened letter-spacing (+0.04em) to deliver instant visual triage across dense data tables.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded by a maximum dashboard width of 1440px to preserve data readability across ultra-wide monitors.

- **Grid Behavior:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) outer canvas margins. Side navigation rails pin at a fixed 260px, while agent copilot drawers dock at a collapsible 380px.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 24px (`1.5rem`) margins. Copilot transforms into an overlay sheet; primary metrics collapse into a 2x2 modular matrix.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 16px (`1rem`) margins. Navigation shifts from horizontal top/side rails to an Obsidian bottom app dock.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal spacing adheres to a strict 4px base multiplier. Dense tabular interfaces use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for inline metric tags, while analytical dashboard sections distribute cards via `space-lg` to prevent visual fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through ultra-fine boundary definition and ambient, non-muddy luminescence rather than stacked dark drops.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground color `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, zero shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Deck):** Pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`) bounded by a single-pixel structural hairline of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls):** Elevated panels sustain a soft ambient plume: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)` with borders shifting subtly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Command Palettes, Popovers & Floating Trays):** High-level overlay surfaces feature crisp bounding: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with backdrop blur filters of `8px` when resting over complex charts.
- **AI Focal Glow (Kinetic Illumination):** Autonomous actions and dynamic agent streams avoid standard shadows, applying an ambient aura: `0 0 0 1px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25), 0 4px 12px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`.

## Shapes
Surfaces use a consistent **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature with a strict 12px (`0.75rem`) baseline for operational cards, data modals, and contextual panels.

- **Cards & Data Modules:** 12px border radius ensures a modern, tactile enclosure while maximizing inner space for ledger entries and analytics charts.
- **Input Fields & Form Controls:** Fixed at 8px (`0.5rem`) to remain sharp, functional, and aligned with standard numeric keypads.
- **Chips, Badges & Interactive Status Tags:** Fully curved pill geometries (`9999px`) provide contrast against grid-aligned rectilinear data structures.
- **Navigation Dock & Bottom Anchors:** Outer corners match viewport boundaries, with inner elevated elements curved at 12px.

## Components

### Header & Navigation
- **Top / Dock Navigation:** Solid Obsidian Black (`#0F172A`). Brand lockups and active items feature pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with inactive routes staged at `#94A3B8`. 
- **Active Navigation Indicator:** Highlighted with an Electric Indigo (`#4F46E5`) subtle base pill or bottom line accent (2px height).

### Buttons
- **Primary Actions:** Electric Indigo background (`#4F46E5`), high-contrast white text (`#FFFFFF`), 8px border radius, font weight 600. Hover state shifts to `#4338CA` with an active press scale of `0.98`.
- **Secondary Actions:** Obsidian Slate background (`#0F172A`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), 8px radius. Hover state shifts to `#1E293B`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Actions:** Transparent ground, border of `#E2E8F0`, slate typography (`#334155`). Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9`.
- **AI Kinetic Action Button:** Soft Indigo ground (`#E0E7FF`), Deep Indigo text (`#4338CA`), framed with an ultra-thin border tinted in Luminous Mint (`#10B981`). Accompanied by a pulsing spark glyph.

### Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface fill, 12px border radius, enclosed by a 1px solid hairline border of `#E2E8F0`. 
- **Padding:** 24px (`space-lg`) on desktop; 16px (`space-md`) on mobile viewports.
- **AI Summary Cards:** Pure white surface with an integrated left-border accent (3px width) in Luminous Mint (`#10B981`), backed by an ultra-subtle vertical gradient fading from `#F0FDF4` to `#FFFFFF` at the top 15% of the card.

### Chips & Badges
- **Verified / Settled:** Soft Mint background (`#D1FAE5`), Emerald text (`#059669`), `label-sm` semibold uppercase, padding `4px 10px`, pill-shaped.
- **Flagged / Variance:** Soft Peach background (`#FFEDD5`), Burnt Orange text (`#C2410C`), `label-sm` semibold uppercase, padding `4px 10px`, pill-shaped.
- **GenAI Agent Actions:** Soft Indigo background (`#E0E7FF`), Deep Indigo text (`#4338CA`), `label-sm` semibold, pill-shaped, decorated with an inline 6px dot in Luminous Mint (`#10B981`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** White ground (`#FFFFFF`), 1px border of `#CBD5E1`, 8px border radius, height 44px, text set in `#0F172A` with placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border of `#4F46E5` accompanied by an immediate focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px dimensions, 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Unchecked border `#CBD5E1`; checked fill `#4F46E5` with a crisp white tick mark.

### Data Tables & Transaction Rows
- **Row Styling:** Alternating hover highlight using `#F8FAFC`. Base row border bottom 1px solid `#F1F5F9`.
- **Ledger Figures:** Right-aligned, formatted with `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`. Positive inflows prefixed with high-contrast text; outbound flows distinctly categorized.

### GenAI Command & Prompt Interface
- **Prompt Drawer:** Persistent or floating Obsidian command pill with rounded-full geometry, `#0F172A` background, containing an inner glass field (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), white text entry, and a dedicated `#10B981` execution trigger.
